NexFlow Logistics - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

1. Overview

NexFlow Logistics ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you engage with our operational dispatch communication system.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal and operational information necessary to facilitate commercial freight transportation and dispatch coordination, including driver name, direct mobile telephone number, email address, assigned truck/trailer unit numbers, pickup and delivery verification logs, and load confirmation receipts.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely for operational logistics purposes, including sending automated SMS load assignments, rate confirmation summaries, pickup/delivery instructions, and coordinating transit schedule adjustments.

4. SMS / Mobile Opt-In Data Clause (Strict Non-Sharing Policy)

NexFlow Logistics does not sell, rent, lease, or share mobile telephone numbers or SMS opt-in consent data.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with or disclosed to any third parties or affiliates under any circumstances.

5. Data Security & Retention

We maintain administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
